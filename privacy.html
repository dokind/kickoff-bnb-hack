Cypher Run privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

Cypher Run is a hackathon project that runs on BNB Smart Chain testnet. It uses test money only: nothing in it has real value. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no ads and no tracking.

What stays on your device

  • Your wallet's private key. It is made on your device and stored only there (in the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore, or your browser's storage). We never receive it.
  • Whether you have seen the welcome screens.

What reaches our server

  • Your wallet address, when the app asks for a round's state or sends a check-in.
  • On a check-in day: the date and today's step count. On a phone the count is read from Apple Health or Health Connect, with your permission; in a browser you type it. We keep the count, its source and the time, so the server can sign that you showed up that day. Nothing else is read from your health data, and nothing is written to it.
  • When the app asks the faucet for test BNB to pay network fees: your wallet address and the network address the request came from, kept to limit how often one network can ask.

What is public

Joining a round, each check-in, launches and claims are transactions on BNB Smart Chain testnet. Like every blockchain transaction they are public and permanent: anyone can see the wallet address, the round and the day. They do not include your name, your step count or your health data.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share data, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • We do not use health data for anything except deciding whether a day counts.
  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, location or contacts.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app (or clearing the site's data) deletes the key on your device. To have the server forget your wallet's check-in records, open an issue on the project's GitHub page with the wallet address. Records already on the blockchain cannot be removed by anyone.
